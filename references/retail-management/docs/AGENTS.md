# Documentation guidance

- Preserve whether a document is current, draft, proposed, accepted or historical.
- Record a replacement decision with its date and link instead of rewriting old meeting records.
- Keep PlantUML source beside generated diagrams; update both when a diagram changes.
- Do not claim team, lecturer or reviewer approval without evidence.
- Use repository-relative links and check them after moving files.
- Product status belongs in the current sprint/backlog; historical context belongs in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` or dated meeting notes.
